"With You" — Psychology-Powered Relationship App

Southwest University 2025 Innovation Project · No. X202510345034

Overview

"With You" is a psychology-informed game software designed to apply evidence-based psychological principles to romantic relationship development and marital wellbeing. The project was funded as a Southwest University 2025 University-level Innovation & Entrepreneurship Training Program (项目编号: X202510345034).

The core idea: rather than "fixing" problems after they arise, this app supports couples in building psychological safety, healthy communication patterns, and emotional literacy through engaging, game-based interactions.

Background

Relationship dissatisfaction and marital breakdown are significant sources of psychological distress. While couples therapy is effective, it is expensive, stigmatized, and often initiated too late. Digital tools designed for prevention — rather than remediation — remain underdeveloped.

This project was also recognized at the 2025 Psychology Product Design Forum (心理学产品设计分论坛) at Zhejiang Normal University, where it received the 2nd Prize, validating the concept's potential as a psychologically grounded product.

Project Development

Phase 1

Theoretical Grounding

Identified core psychological constructs to embed: attachment styles, communication patterns, love languages, cognitive reframing.

  • Literature review on relationship psychology
  • Mapped theory to game mechanics
  • Consulted with clinical supervisors
Phase 2

Design & Prototyping

Designed game scenarios, interaction flows, and feedback mechanisms grounded in psychological principles.

  • Created wireframes and user flows
  • Designed scenario-based challenge modules
  • Iterated based on peer feedback
Phase 3

Development & Evaluation

Built working prototype; evaluating psychological validity and user experience.

  • Prototype built (current stage)
  • User testing in progress
  • Planning formal efficacy evaluation
